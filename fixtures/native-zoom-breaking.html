Native-zoom-breaking fixture

A complex page deliberately designed to break under native reflow zoom: the root scroll area is disabled, the sticky nav has a pixel-pinned minimum width, the fixed modal has a fixed width, and the canvas is rasterised once at load. Control+Plus / Control+Minus reflows the layout without re-measuring, so the fixed and sticky geometry gets clipped and the raster content degrades.
